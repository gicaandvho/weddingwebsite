## 1. Overall Website Direction

The website should feel primarily like an elegant digital wedding
invitation, with the gaming concept appearing through subtle microcopy,
pixel illustrations, small interactions, and easter eggs.

### Core Design Principles

-   Light cream or warm-white backgrounds should dominate.
-   Navy blue is the primary typography color.
-   Burgundy is used for CTAs, hearts, selected states, and important
    accents.
-   Muted gold is reserved for thin dividers and restrained ornamental
    details.
-   Elegant serif typography should be used for names and major wedding
    headings.
-   Pixel-style typography should only appear in small game/system
    labels and microcopy.
-   Pixel art should remain sparse and decorative.
-   Maintain generous whitespace.
-   Keep all important information easy to read, including for elderly
    guests.
-   Avoid heavy frames, thick pixel borders, full game HUDs, and large
    dark panels.
-   Animations should generally last around 0.5--1 second and should
    never block access to information.

### Navigation

Desktop navigation:

**G&V \| HOME \| WEDDING DETAILS ▾ \| GUEST GUIDE ▾ \| GALLERY \| RSVP**

Mobile navigation:

-   Hamburger menu
-   Full-screen or spacious mobile drawer
-   No bottom navigation

Navigation labels should remain practical and obvious. Gaming
terminology belongs mainly in page content and microcopy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What time should I arrive?

> We recommend arriving by **9:30 AM or earlier** to give you enough
> time to park and settle in. The wedding ceremony will **strictly begin
> at 10:00 AM**, so please plan accordingly.

### Can I take photos or videos during the ceremony?

> We're having an **unplugged ceremony** and kindly ask that phones and
> cameras be kept away during the ceremony. Our photographers and
> videographers will be there to capture the moments, so we invite you
> to simply be present and celebrate with us. ♡

### When is the RSVP deadline?

> Kindly send your response on or before **January 16, 2027**. Your
> timely RSVP will help us finalize our seating and other wedding
> arrangements.

### What kind of food and drinks will be served?

> A buffet-style meal and refreshments will be served during the
> reception. If you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions,
> please let us know when you RSVP so we can coordinate accordingly.

Do not reveal cocktail-hour food carts or detailed menu surprises here.

### Are children allowed?

> Our celebration will be primarily adults-only. Children who are
> specifically invited will be indicated on the invitation. We kindly
> ask that only the guests named on your invitation attend.

### Can I bring a plus one?

> Plus ones are included for selected invitations. Please check your
> RSVP for the number of seats reserved for your party.

### Is parking available?

> Yes. Parking is available at both the ceremony church and the
> reception venue. We recommend arriving a little early to allow enough
> time to park and settle in comfortably.

### What should I wear?

Use a short response directing guests to the Outfit Guide.

**View Outfit Guide →**

### What if I can't attend?

> We'll be sad not to celebrate with you, but we completely understand.
> Please let us know through the RSVP so we can adjust our seating
> accordingly. Even if you can't be there on the day, we're truly
> grateful to have you as part of our lives.

### Who can I contact on the wedding day?

> For any questions or concerns on the wedding day, please contact our
> wedding coordinator.
