Our Story

From an unexpected meeting to a life they’re building together.

Gica and Vho first crossed paths toward the end of 2024. A few encounters later, a conversation on Gica’s birthday became the beginning of something more.

What followed was a collection of adventures—traveling with family to the UAE, experiencing Vho’s first BLACKPINK concert, exploring Japan together, and discovering that life was simply more fun when they shared it.

Along the way, Vho became more willing to step outside his comfort zone, while Gica found someone ready to share her adventurous spirit. Somewhere between the big adventures and ordinary days, they began seeing the same future.

Then came a proposal, a yes, and the start of their greatest adventure yet.
