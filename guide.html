FAQ & Gifts

Everything else you might want to know.

A Little Note About Our Guest List

We would truly love to celebrate with everyone, but with limited seating available, we hope you understand that we can only accommodate the guests included in each invitation.

What time should I arrive?

We recommend arriving by 9:30 AM or earlier to give you enough time to park and settle in. The wedding ceremony will strictly begin at 10:00 AM, so please plan accordingly.

Can I take photos or videos during the ceremony?

We're having an unplugged ceremony and kindly ask that phones and cameras be kept away during the ceremony. Our photographers and videographers will be there to capture the moments, so we invite you to simply be present and celebrate with us. ♡

When is the RSVP deadline?

Kindly send your response on or before January 16, 2027. Your timely RSVP will help us finalize our seating and other wedding arrangements.

What kind of food and drinks will be served?

A buffet-style meal and refreshments will be served during the reception. If you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions, please let us know when you RSVP so we can coordinate accordingly.

Are children allowed?

Our celebration will be primarily adults-only. Children who are specifically invited will be indicated on the invitation. We kindly ask that only the guests named on your invitation attend.

Can I bring a plus one?

Plus ones are included for selected invitations. Please check your RSVP for the number of seats reserved for your party.

Is parking available?

Yes. Parking is available at both the ceremony church and the reception venue. We recommend arriving a little early to allow enough time to park and settle in comfortably.

What should I wear?

Please see our Outfit Guide for the dress code, colors, and attire inspiration. View Outfit Guide →

What if I can't attend?

We'll be sad not to celebrate with you, but we completely understand. Please let us know through the RSVP so we can adjust our seating accordingly. Even if you can't be there on the day, we're truly grateful to have you as part of our lives.

Who can I contact on the wedding day?

For any questions or concerns on the wedding day, please contact our wedding coordinator. Contact details to follow.
