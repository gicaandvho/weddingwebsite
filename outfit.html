Outfit Guide

Dress for the celebration.

Guests Wedding Entourage

Dress Code

Semi-Formal to Smart Casual

  • Charcoal Clay #3A2B20
  • Rustic Taupe #7E6A52
  • Warm Beige #B89C82

Please wear colors within or close to the tones above. Exact match is not required. We simply ask that your attire falls within these neutral earth-tone shades to create a cohesive and elegant celebration.

Similar shades are welcome, including chocolate brown, espresso, mocha, mushroom taupe, camel, sand, champagne beige, and latte.

Please Avoid

White, Ivory, or Cream · Bright or Neon Colors · Bold Prints and Patterns

For Women

  • An earth-tone midi dress
  • Comfortable dress shoes

For Men

  • A collared shirt and tailored trousers
  • Dress shoes
